# Theriyum — Scenes

## Purpose

This file records music scenes, communities, collectives, and cultural networks and connects them to artists, songs, genres, sounds, and eras within Project Theriyum.

## Scene Information

Each scene entry should eventually include:

- Scene name
- Location or origin
- Era
- Related genres
- Related artists
- Related songs
- Sound characteristics
- Collectives or communities
- Theriyum notes

---

## Modern Phonk Underground

### Scene Information

- Scene name: Modern Phonk Underground
- Era: 2010s–2020s
- Related genres: Phonk, Trap, Wave, Bass music
- Related artists: BACKWHEN
- Related songs: BACKWHEN — Purity

### Sound Characteristics

- Heavy bass
- Atmospheric production
- Electronic textures
- Trap-influenced rhythms
- Sample-driven production

### Collectives or Communities

- Purple Posse

### Theriyum Notes


- BACKWHEN is connected to the modern phonk movement through Purple Posse.
- The scene connects Phonk with Trap, Wave, Bass Music, and electronic production.
- Purity provides a song-level connection between BACKWHEN and this scene.
- Theriyum can use scenes to connect artists and sounds that share a musical community even when they do not share the same genre.
