## 1990s Memphis Rap

### Era Information

- Era name: 1990s Memphis Rap
- Approximate time period: 1990s
- Related genres: Memphis rap, underground hip-hop
- Related artists: Three 6 Mafia, DJ Spanish Fly, Tommy Wright III
- Sound characteristics: Dark samples, heavy bass, chopped vocal samples, atmospheric production, repetitive rhythms
- Scene or culture: Memphis underground hip-hop

### Musical Developments

- Dark and sample-driven production became an important part of the Memphis underground sound.
- Production techniques and musical ideas from this era later became influential in the development of modern phonk.

### Theriyum Notes

- This era represents an important historical root connected to the later Phonk network.
- Theriyum can use this era to connect older Memphis sounds with modern artists and songs.
